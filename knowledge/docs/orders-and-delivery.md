# Orders and delivery

## Ordering
Order in the shop, by phone on 555-0142 or by email to hello@cornerbakery.example. Small orders are ready in 20 minutes. Custom cakes need 48 hours' notice and a 30% deposit.

## Delivery
We deliver within 5 km of the shop, Tuesday to Saturday 9:00 to 16:00. Delivery costs $4 and is free over $40. Order by 11:00 for same-day delivery.

## Catering
Office catering for 10 or more people with 3 days' notice. Pastry platters start at $45.

## Problems
If an order is wrong, tell us within 24 hours and we will replace the item or refund it.
