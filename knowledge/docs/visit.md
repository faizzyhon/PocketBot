# Visit Corner Bakery

## Finding us
12 Mill Lane, Riverton, on the corner by the bus stop. Bus 14 stops outside. There are 4 free parking spaces behind the shop, entrance from Church Street.

## Opening hours
Monday to Saturday 7:00 to 18:00. Sunday 8:00 to 14:00. Public holidays 8:00 to 13:00. Closed Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

## Inside
24 seats inside and 12 on the patio. Dogs are welcome on the patio only. Free Wi-Fi for customers. Students get 10% off on weekdays after 14:00.
